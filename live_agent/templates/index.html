🟢 Live paper trading

Hand the agents some paper capital and let them trade the live market on their own — opening/closing positions from STRONG signals, with stops, targets and (simulated) leverage + liquidation. Watch the equity move in real time. Crypto runs 24/7; NSE intraday runs only 09:15–15:30 IST and squares off at the close.
Simulated money only (up to 125x, like Binance, so you can SEE liquidation). At your leverage a position is liquidated on a ~80.0% adverse move. The circuit breaker squares off & halts the whole session if it loses your stop-out %.
Configure and press Start. Status updates live.

🏛️ Committee of 45 agents

45 independent setups (trend · momentum · mean-reversion · breakout · volume · statistical) each commit to BUY (long) or SELL (short) — no fence-sitting. The committee acts only when at least the threshold agree. Higher threshold = rarer, higher-conviction.

🔭 Multi-timeframe alignment

How pros structure a trade (Elder's Triple Screen): the higher timeframe sets the allowed direction, lower timeframes time the entry, and disagreement = no trade. The 45 agents are re-weighted per frame — mean-reversion & volume lead on 5m/15m, trend & momentum lead on 4h/1d. One clear verdict instead of 45 votes.
Enter a symbol and press Analyze.
